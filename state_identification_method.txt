Sprint Start Phases (Sagittal View)


GET (timestamp 00:15) (FRAME: 452)
 
Also called “on the blocks” position.

Key posture cues:

Hands on the ground, shoulder width apart, fingers pointing forward

Head slightly down

One knee (usually the rear leg) on the ground, the other leg forward with foot on the block

Hips low, behind the shoulders but above the knee of the rear leg


SET (timestamp 00:19)(FRAME: 479)



Ready position just before the gun.

Key posture cues:

Hands still on the ground

Both knees slightly off the ground, hips raised a bit

Torso slightly forward, preparing to explode forward

Weight shifted forward onto the front leg


GO (timestamp 00:19)(FRAME: 495)



Push-off / start of the sprint.

Key posture cues:

Hands leave the ground

Body rises and leans forward

Legs start driving backward and forward (first stride)

Shoulders and head rise slightly compared to GET/SET
